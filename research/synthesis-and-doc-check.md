## Check against Grand_Opening_Cafe_Instructions.docx

Theme must come from the starter list or be original, and must pass the Grandmother & Employer Test. The site is static: no forms, JS, bookings, fake reviews or invented "since" claims. It needs 6+ menu items (3 drinks, 2 food, 2 themed names), a recurring event, 3+ house rules, and 7 days of hours with one unusual day.

| Check | Botanica → Fernlight (Nature/plant café) | Cartridge & Cup (Retro arcade) | Study Hall (Study café) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Matches a starter theme | Yes: Nature / plant café | Yes: Retro arcade café | Yes: Study café |
