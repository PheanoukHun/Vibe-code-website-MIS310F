# Project: Grand Opening Café (MIS 302F)

Goal: a two-page static website (index.html, menu.html, style.css, images/) for a themed Austin café, hosted on Neocities. Requirements live in `Grand_Opening_Cafe_Instructions.docx`; the evidence tracker is `Grand_Opening_Cafe_Evidence_Tracker.docx`.

## Layout
- `research/`: concept research (ideas, existence checks, viability, synthesis)
- `marketing/`: tagline and marketing copy
- `site/`: the website files that get uploaded to Neocities

## Working rules
- Research uses separate sub-agents for ideation, existence checks and viability; their outputs are saved as markdown in `research/`.
- The site must stay static: no JavaScript, forms, ordering or fake reviews/awards/"since" claims.
- Contact info is fictional: phone (512) 555-01XX, email ending `.example`.
- Never claim health benefits; never use trademarks, famous characters or real-people photos.
- Part 7 reflections are written by the student with no AI. Don't draft them.
