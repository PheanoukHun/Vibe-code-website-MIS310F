## One-line pitch
A sunlit greenhouse café in Austin serving coffee, tea and matcha among the ferns.

## Welcome paragraph (Home page, 5 sentences)
Fernlight is a greenhouse café where the seating is tucked between ferns, hanging planters and tall glass windows. We serve coffee, tea and hand-whisked matcha, along with pastries and light bites baked to go with them. It's a good fit for students, remote workers, friends catching up, and anyone who wants a bright place to slow down. Settle in at a long table or a quiet corner and let the greenery do the decorating. Come by for one cup and don't be surprised if you stay for two.

## Signature drinks and bites (themed names, to adopt into your brief)
- **Fern Fog Latte**: espresso with steamed milk and a cold-foam "mist"
- **Greenhouse Whisk**: ceremonial-style matcha, whisked to order
- **Sun Room Cold Brew**: cold brew with orange peel and honey
- **Moss Cloud Matcha Lemonade**: sparkling, iced
- **Terrarium Toast**: seasonal fruit and herbed ricotta on sourdough
- **Fern Frond Scone**: brown-butter scone with lemon glaze

## Recurring event
**Saturday Whisk Workshop**: learn to whisk your own matcha. Walk-ins welcome (no signup).
