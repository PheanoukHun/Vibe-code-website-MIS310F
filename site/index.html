Welcome

Fernlight is a greenhouse café where the seating is tucked between ferns, hanging planters, and tall glass windows. We serve coffee, tea, and hand-whisked matcha, along with pastries and light bites baked to go with them. It's a good fit for students, remote workers, friends catching up, and anyone who wants a bright place to slow down. Settle in at a long table or a quiet corner and let the greenery do the decorating. Come by for one cup, and don't be surprised if you stay for two.
